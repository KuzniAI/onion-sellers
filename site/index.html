How ze dish is scored: quality is each category's mix of Artificial Analysis indexes, in index points. Score is that quality minus a penalty for each doubling of cost above $1 per task and of response time above 60 s; the rates are shown under each category. Each model is listed at its best-scoring reasoning effort, shown after its name. $/task is what one Intelligence Index task would cost at the provider's prices, so it counts how many tokens a model spends; Time is seconds until a 500-token answer is complete, thinking included; $/1M is the blended per-token price (3 output tokens per input token).
